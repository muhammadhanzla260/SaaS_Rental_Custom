Moving systems feels risky because the old one, however messy, works. The trick is to move in a fixed order and check the money before anything goes live.

## 1. Export what you have

Products, prices and customers from your spreadsheet or current system, as CSV. Don't clean it up first; that is what the import step is for.

## 2. Map and import it

Each product needs its units, its price per service type (staffed, drop-off, pick-up) and its included hours. Customers come with their saved addresses.

## 3. Check prices to the cent

Re-price a handful of recent orders in the new system and compare them with what you actually charged. Differences point at a missing rule: travel, overtime, a surface fee.

> We can do this step with you and check every price before you go live.

## 4. Go live on a quiet day

Turn on online quotes and payments when you are ready, ideally midweek. Bring upcoming bookings across first so the calendar is complete from day one.
