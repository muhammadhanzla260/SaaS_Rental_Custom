> **In plain words:** We collect what we need to run Rent Hatch, never sell it, and let you export or delete it.

## What we collect

Account details, your business data, usage data and crew location only while en route.

## How we use it

To run the service, provide support and improve the product.

## Your customers' data

You are the controller; we act as your processor under the DPA.

## Sharing

Only with the sub-processors listed on our Security page.

## Retention

How long data is kept, including 90 days for GPS pings.

## Your rights

Access, correction, export and deletion, under GDPR, UK GDPR and US state laws.
